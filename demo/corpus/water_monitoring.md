# Water Monitoring
Sensors and the thresholds that trigger action.
    </header>

    <section id="sensors">
## Sensors
* Three Aquaprobe T3 sondes sit on lines L2, L8 and L13. They log temperature, salinity and nitrate every 15 minutes.
* Each sonde is recalibrated every 4 weeks.
    </section>

    <section id="thresholds">
## Thresholds
* If water stays above 14 °C for 3 days in a row, the board considers harvesting early.
* Nitrate under 2 µM for two weeks means slow growth is likely.
* Salinity below 24 psu after a storm means watching blades for bleaching.
