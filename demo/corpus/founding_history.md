How the cooperative started.
    </header>

    <section id="history">
## Timeline
* March 2019: Ines Okafor-Vale, a former ferry deckhand, and Dmitri Haldane, a retired marine biologist, called the first meeting in the Wren Cove boathouse. Nine founding members each paid a $500 share.
* September 2019: the group bought a used 11.2 m boat, the Pelican, for $18,500.
* November 2019: first seeding, on 4 longlines.
* Spring 2020: first harvest, 4.1 tonnes wet weight, sold for $350 a tonne.
* 2022: six more longlines (L9 to L14) brought the total to 14.
