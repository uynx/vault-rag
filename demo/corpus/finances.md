## Numbers
* Wet kelp sold to the main alginate buyer for $410 a tonne in 2024 and $480 a tonne in 2025.
* 2025 gross revenue was $41,300 against $33,900 in costs, leaving a surplus of $7,400.
* Membership dues are $240 per member per year.
* In March 2022 the cooperative borrowed $85,000 from Northline Credit Union at 5.2% over 10 years, about $910 a month, to build the drying shed and add lines L9 to L14.
* Annual gear maintenance is budgeted at $6,500 ([[gear_maintenance]]).
