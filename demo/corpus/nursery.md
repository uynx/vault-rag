# Wren Cove Nursery
Where seed twine is grown.
    </header>

    <section id="setup">
## Setup
* Six 1,000 L tanks. Fertile kelp tissue is collected from wild plants in September and spores are released into the tanks.
* Gametophytes grow for 4 to 6 weeks at 10 °C under blue-filtered LED light before the twine is seeded.
    </section>

    <section id="contamination">
## October 2023 contamination
* A diatom bloom got into tank 4 and spread to two more tanks, so three of the six batches were thrown away.
* Staff reseeded from tank 2, which had been kept isolated, and deployment slipped to November 14.
* This is the main reason the 2024 harvest was smaller than the 2025 one. Tanks now get a weekly microscope check.
