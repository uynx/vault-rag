# Lower Basin Expansion
The plan to add ten more longlines.
    </header>

## Plan
* Add 10 longlines in the Lower Basin by 2027 on an 8 acre lease.
* The lease application was filed on February 11, 2026.
* Estimated cost is $210,000: a $120,000 grant from the Coastal Futures Fund, a $60,000 new loan and a $30,000 levy on members.
* Members approved the plan in March 2026, 24 votes for, 5 against and 2 abstaining.
* If it goes through the cooperative will run 24 longlines, but the two boats may not keep up ([[boats]]).
