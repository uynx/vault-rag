## Harvest
* The window runs from mid-April to early June. Crews of 4 cut blades at about 3 m long.
* The most the crews have landed in one day is 4.5 tonnes wet weight.
* Yearly wet-weight totals: 2024 gave 38.2 tonnes, 2025 gave 52.4 tonnes and 2026 gave 57.9 tonnes.
* The 2026 harvest ended on June 2. The target had been 60 tonnes.
* Loads are carried to the Wren Cove dock by Gannet and Skerry.
