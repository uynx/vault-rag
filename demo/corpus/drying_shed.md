# Drying Shed
Processing wet kelp into flakes.
    </header>

    <section id="shed">
## Shed
* Built in 2022 at Wren Cove, 40 m² of floor.
* The dehydrator takes up to 600 kg of wet kelp per day and dries it at 45 °C for 14 hours, down to 12% moisture.
* Wet to dry weight is about 10 to 1.
* Solar air preheaters cut the electricity bill during the spring harvest.
* Roughly one fifth of each harvest is dried here; the rest leaves wet ([[buyers]]).
