# Members and Governance
Who decides what.
    </header>

    <section id="rules">
## Rules
* Each member has one vote, whatever number of shares they hold. A share costs $500 and a new member needs a current member as sponsor.
* A general assembly meets every January and elects a board of five.
* Quorum is 12 members. Changing the bylaws takes two thirds of the members present.
* Every member owes 40 volunteer hours a year.
    </section>
