## Outreach
* Six school visits a year, usually in April.
* Open Harvest Day is held on the first Saturday of May. In 2025 it drew 120 visitors.
* Harlow Marine Lab, led by Dr. Amara Quist, takes monthly nitrate samples next to the sondes ([[water_monitoring]]) and shares the results.
* A monthly newsletter called The Dropper goes to members and sponsors.
