# Longlines
Layout of the growing lines in the Upper Basin.
    </header>

    <section id="layout">
## Layout
* 14 longlines, numbered L1 to L14. L1 to L8 date from 2019 to 2021 and L9 to L14 were added in 2022.
* Each line is 120 m long and runs east to west to match tidal current, which peaks near 0.6 m/s.
* Both ends are held by 40 kg helical screw anchors, each rated for a 1.8 tonne pull.
* Droppers hang every 1.5 m and hold the seeded twine at a depth of 2 m.
* L10 to L14 sit in the shallower end of the basin and are the first to warm in spring.
