## Process
* Seed spools are wound at the nursery ([[nursery]]) starting in late September.
* Lines go into the water in the first week of November, once the water drops below 12 °C. In 2025 deployment began on November 4.
* Twine carries about 300 juvenile kelp per metre.
* Two boats and 11 volunteers can seed one line in about 90 minutes, so the full set of 14 lines takes roughly 4 working days.
* A late seeding costs growth: the 2023 deployment slipped to November 14 after the nursery contamination ([[nursery]]).
