## Fleet
* **Gannet**: 9.4 m aluminium workboat with an 80 hp diesel. It hauls 2.0 tonnes per trip. Bought in 2021 for $41,000.
* **Skerry**: 7.8 m skiff with a 10 kW electric outboard. It hauls 0.9 tonnes per trip and has about 5 hours of range. Bought in 2023.
* **Pelican**: 11.2 m, 150 hp, hauled 3.5 tonnes per trip. Retired in August 2025 after a hull survey found corrosion along the keel, and sold for scrap at $6,200.
* With Pelican gone, the two remaining boats move 2.9 tonnes per round of trips.
