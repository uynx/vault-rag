## Split of a typical harvest
* Corvane Foods takes 60% for alginate extraction under a contract that runs through 2027.
* Saltmarsh Kitchen, a restaurant supplier, takes 15% as fresh blades.
* Eastgate Fertilizer takes the 5% of offcuts at $60 a tonne.
* The last 20% goes to the drying shed ([[drying_shed]]) and becomes the cooperative's own products ([[products]]).
