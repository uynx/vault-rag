# Safety Protocol
Rules for every trip on the water.
    </header>

    <section id="rules">
## Rules
* Life jackets are mandatory, and no boat leaves with fewer than two people aboard.
* Boats stay in port when forecast wind is above 22 knots. The limit was 25 knots until March 2024.
* Crews check in on VHF channel 68 every 2 hours.
    </section>

    <section id="incident">
## Skerry capsize, February 2024
* The Skerry rolled in a 1.2 m chop. Both crew were pulled out within 6 minutes and nobody was hurt.
* The board lowered the wind limit from 25 to 22 knots in response.
