# May 2025 Heatwave
The warm spell that cut the 2025 harvest short.
    </header>

    <section id="events">
## What happened
* Water passed 14 °C on May 17 and peaked at 15.3 °C on May 19, 2025.
* President Priya Nandakumar ordered an early harvest on May 18. It finished on May 28 instead of the planned June 6.
* About 6 tonnes of blades rotted before they could be cut, mostly on L10 to L14 ([[longlines]]). Without that loss the season would have come close to 58 tonnes.
* Afterwards the board added a rule: the 14 °C threshold in [[water_monitoring]] now triggers a board call within 24 hours.
